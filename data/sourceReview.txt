Source review for Eden's catalog continuation
Checked October 29, 2026

Venture X Rewards
https://www.capitalone.com/credit-cards/venture-x/
The individual page lists a $395 annual fee, variable purchase APR from
19.74% to 28.74%, no foreign transaction fee, two miles per dollar normally,
ten on qualifying portal hotels/cars and five on portal flights/vacation
rentals. Entertainment purchases through its service have five miles per
dollar. These are earning units, not cash. Lounge enrollment, guest rules,
hotel benefits and credits are not automatically counted as cash value.

Discover it Miles
https://www.discover.com/credit-cards/travel/
The page lists 1.5 miles per dollar and no annual fee. Its footnote bases the
new-cardholder match on the first 365 days and processing dates, with excluded
rewards and closed accounts. Redemption values may change. The calculator
uses the normal rate and requires the user's cash-value assumption.

Wells Fargo Active Cash
https://creditcards.wellsfargo.com/active-cash-credit-card/
The product page lists two percent on eligible purchases and no annual fee.
Its introductory APR lasts twelve months; published ongoing choices are
18.74%, 24.74% or 28.74%. The source record stores the bounds and explains
the actual choices in its notes. Offer channels can differ. Transfer fees,
cash advances and account-specific pricing still need the linked disclosure.
An unknown foreign fee has not been filled with zero.

Wells Fargo Autograph
https://creditcards.wellsfargo.com/autograph-visa-credit-card/
The page lists no annual or foreign transaction fee. Eligible dining, travel,
gas/charging, transit, popular streaming and phone plans earn three points
per dollar; other eligible purchases earn one. The purchase intro period is
twelve months, followed by the listed ongoing APR choices. Merchant coding
and category definitions apply. Insurance coverage is not a cash credit and
has not been automatically added to yearly value.

These notes are short summaries of primary product pages. Following links to
two separate Wells Fargo pricing disclosures was unavailable during this check.
No missing disclosure values were guessed. A source
date is a snapshot, not a promise that future terms or a personal offer match.
