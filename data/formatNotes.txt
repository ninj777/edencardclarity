Catalog source files

These are UTF-8 tab-separated text files. Keep the first line unchanged.
Each record is one line; tabs separate fields, including empty fields.
A line beginning with # is a comment. A single dash means an unknown SQL
value only in nullable fields. An empty string means an empty text field.
Do not put tabs, newlines or hidden nulls inside a field.

cardRecords.tsv has these fields, in order:
  key, issuer, name, network, country, currency, reward unit,
  first-year fee, ongoing fee, minimum APR, maximum APR, foreign fee,
  check date, checked flag, archived flag, aliases, summary, source URL.

Keys are permanent product/version identities. Do not reuse a key for another
card. Country and currency are codes such as US and USD. Network may be blank
if it has not been checked. Reward units are 0 cashback, 1 points, 2 miles,
3 no supported reward rate. Fees use cents; APR and foreign fees use basis
points, so 1874 means 18.74 percent. A dash stays unknown, not zero.
Dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Flags use 0 or 1. Aliases are separated by |.
Checked means the supported key fields were checked, not that every account
agreement or personal offer was verified. An issuer-list source normally
supports the name only, so those records stay unchecked.

rewardRules.tsv fields:
  rule key, card key, category, channel, total rate, cap key,
  activation/condition flag, fallback rate, start date, end date, notes, source.

Rates use millionths. For cashback, 30000 means 3 percent. For points or miles,
3000000 means three units per dollar. These are total rates, not amounts to
add to a base rate. Channels currently include any, direct, portal and online.
Portal means the specific issuer service named in the rule notes. A blank
start/end is unbounded. Eligibility or expiring promotions without enough
structured information stay in notes; do not pretend they are modeled.

The category other is the general base rule. A selected rule uses the wallet's
manually confirmed category. It supports one actual selected category at a
time; it is not a guess about the bank's quarterly list. See rewardMath.c for
the category names accepted by the menus. Each card has at most 40 rules.

capGroups.tsv fields:
  cap key, card key, spending limit in cents, period, notes, source.
Period numbers are 1 calendar month, 2 calendar quarter, 3 calendar year,
4 anniversary year, 5 statement cycle. Multiple rules share the same cap key
when they share one spending allowance. This schema models spending caps,
not every possible reward-dollar limit or balance-based tier.

feeNotes.tsv fields:
  card key, fee kind, explanation, source.
Use these notes for introductory periods, transfer conditions, exclusions and
other terms that a single number would hide. The calculator does not turn
these notes into an interest calculation.

benefitRules.tsv fields:
  benefit key, card key, label, possible credit in cents, period,
  activation/condition flag, due date, notes, source.
Credits are possible conditional benefits. They are not automatically added
to yearly value. The user enters what they actually expect to use.

Build a candidate with --build-catalog data candidateCatalog.db. Import runs
inside a separate temporary database. Required files, columns, relationships,
types, dates and index contents are checked before replacement. Keep sources
and review their conditions before changing checked to 1. Software validation
does not check whether the bank changed its offer after our recorded date.

legacy001 through legacy005 are our original invented samples. They remain
archived for old-wallet import. Do not rename them into real products.
