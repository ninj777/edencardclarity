Card terms follow-up
Checked October 30, 2026

This is the second pass through the twenty products. It adds detail to
twentyCardResearch.txt. The first pass left too many fee descriptions broad.
This pass checks pricing tables, sample agreements, rewards agreements,
member guides and the regulator's agreement directory as well as product pages.

How to read the sources

Direct document means the actual issuer page or PDF was readable. Dated
document means its printed date is recorded; the check date does not make it
a new offer. Indexed text means the search service could read an issuer's
public text even though opening the live page left blanks or gave an error.
That text helps fill gaps, but it is not a confirmed live application offer.
Different offer pages and different account versions must stay separate.

No application was submitted and no bank account was accessed. Individual
APRs, credit limits, account amendments, card networks and activation/spending
history cannot all be found in public research. A value which needs one of
those details remains conditional. A calculator marked needs review can also
mean that this version does not collect the necessary inputs.

Remaining checks that require another access route

Open the exact pricing links for Freedom Flex, Sapphire Reserve and Prime
Visa in a normal browser. Confirm each document's date and full APR/fee table.
Then compare any actual account with its own agreement and later notices.
For Citi, compare the currently displayed offer with the older tables and
indexed variants before using its APR. For Capital One and Discover, match
the network, credit version, issuer and agreement. Insurance should be checked
against the guide supplied for that actual account and the proposed purchase.

These are real remaining access and applicability limits. This research does
not establish every personalized contract, future change or claim outcome.
The updated app must preserve those distinctions instead of replacing them
with a claim that all terms are completely verified.
