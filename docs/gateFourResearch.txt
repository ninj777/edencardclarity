What this research covers

We need a benefits catalog, saved-card comparisons, six purchases, ties,
limits and benefit-date notes. Five U.S. personal cashback cards are enough
to demonstrate these rules clearly. This is a checked selection, not a list
of every card or a complete copy of each bank's agreement. The larger catalog
branch is separate and is not the starting point for this work.

The proposal assigns Joe the catalog and expected values, Oliver comparison
functions, Eden stored-record validation and errors, and Evan readable results.
These are responsibility areas, not a record of personal authorship or review.

Capital One Quicksilver Rewards
https://www.capitalone.com/credit-cards/quicksilver/
The individual product page confirms 1.5% on eligible purchases, $0 annual
fee and no foreign transaction fee. Qualifying hotels, rental cars and
vacation rentals through Capital One Travel earn 5%; Capital One Entertainment
purchases also earn 5%. Flights do not inherit that travel bonus. The ordinary
rate is unlimited. Rewards do not expire while the account remains open.
The posted introductory purchase/transfer APR is 0% for 15 months, followed
by 18.74%-28.74% variable. Exact transfer and other pricing details were not
available in the rendered disclosure; they will say to check account terms.
The calculator will not add welcome bonuses or assume free borrowing.

Capital One Savor Rewards
https://www.capitalone.com/credit-cards/savor/
This specific product has $0 annual fee, no foreign transaction fee, 3% on
eligible grocery, dining, entertainment and popular streaming purchases,
and 1% otherwise. Qualifying Capital One Travel hotels/cars/vacation rentals
earn 5%; its Entertainment service earns 8%. Grocery excludes superstores
such as Walmart and Target. Merchant codes and payment processing matter.
The advertised purchase/transfer intro APR lasts 12 months, then the published
variable range is 18.74%-28.74%. Full transfer pricing still needs disclosure.
Do not mix this product with another Savor version or an older annual-fee offer.

Chase Freedom Unlimited
https://creditcards.chase.com/cash-back-credit-cards/freedom/unlimited
https://asset.chase.com/content/dam/card/rulesregulations/en/RPA0509_Web.pdf
The product page and rewards agreement support $0 annual fee, 1.5% base,
3% total dining/drugstores and 5% total on eligible Chase Travel purchases.
Only the amount charged to the card earns rewards, not a part paid with points.
FROSCH bookings are excluded from the Chase Travel bonus. Cash redemption is
one cent per point; other redemption choices can differ. The calculator uses
the stated cash-redemption equivalent, not a speculative travel valuation.
The page currently describes 2% total for qualifying Lyft app purchases through
September 30, 2027. Excluded products do not qualify. After that date we use
the ordinary 1.5% unless later research supports a replacement offer.
DashPass needs activation by December 31, 2029, gives six complimentary months,
and then renews at the current paid rate unless cancelled. Other eligibility
rules apply. These notes are displayed; no subscription value is counted as cash.
The linked current pricing disclosure was blocked. Foreign and transfer fees
will remain unconfirmed; an older or third-party figure will not fill the gap.

American Express Blue Cash Everyday
https://www.americanexpress.com/us/credit-cards/card/blue-cash-everyday/
https://global.americanexpress.com/card-benefits/detail/blue-cash-everyday-321/blue-cash-everyday/
https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-2024-q4-pdf-cmas/cps-lending/blue-cash-everyday-12-31-2024.pdf
The current product page confirms no annual fee, 3% at U.S. supermarkets,
U.S. gas stations and eligible U.S. online retailers, then 1% after $6,000
per year in each category. Each category has its own allowance. The issuer's
indexed benefit terms and older agreement identify calendar-year periods.
Superstores and warehouse clubs do not count as supermarkets. Superstore,
supermarket and warehouse-club gasoline is excluded from the gas bonus.
Online services, travel and dining do not become online retail just because
they were bought online. Use only confirmed eligible purchases in those lanes.
The current page describes an enrolled Disney streaming credit of up to $7
per month on qualifying subscriptions, with auto-renewal. It is not $84 paid
at once or automatic cashback. No credit is added to a purchase estimate.
The benefit dashboard returned 403 when opened directly. Indexed issuer terms
were available. The accessible agreement is explicitly dated December 31,
2024, so its 2.7% foreign fee and APR formula are historical references,
not a verified current personalized offer. Current pricing remains unconfirmed.

Wells Fargo Active Cash
https://creditcards.wellsfargo.com/active-cash-credit-card/

Both the product page and linked current terms were read. They support $0
annual fee and unlimited 2% on eligible purchases. The pricing table lists a
3% foreign transaction fee. Transfers do not earn rewards. The intro transfer
fee within 120 days is the greater of $5 or 3%; afterward up to 5%, minimum $5.

Decisions for the calculator

Use cents for amounts and hundredths of a percent for rates: 300 means 3%.
For an uncapped purchase, reward cents before rounding = amount cents * rate
divided by 10000. Round the final estimate to the nearest cent, half upward.
This is our displayed estimate; a bank can post and round rewards differently.
For a cap, calculate the remaining bonus allowance first, split the purchase,
add both portions, and round once. $30 with $10 left at 3% and $20 at 1%
gives $0.50. Never give the whole purchase 3% or discard its base reward.

Unknown prior category spending must not mean zero. Ask for spending before
this purchase in the purchase's calendar year, or leave it unknown and show
the possible range. A range cannot establish a definite winner. Keep all ties
at the rounded displayed amount; do not silently choose the first card.

Use separate booking choices for direct purchases, Capital One Travel,
Chase Travel and Capital One Entertainment. A generic portal choice would
incorrectly give another bank's bonus. Reject impossible category/channel
combinations. Ask for the purchase date, validate real calendar dates, and
check the Lyft expiration boundary. A source check date is not an offer's
start date and is not proof that today's account terms still match.

The comparisons cover eligible domestic U.S. purchases in USD. Superstores
get their own base-rate category. They do not become supermarkets. Negative,
overlarge, nonnumeric and fractional-cent amounts are rejected. Fees, interest,
cash advances, transfers, cash equivalents and refunds are outside this
purchase estimator. The display must explain that rewards are not net profit.

Keep original IDs 1-5 as practice cards. New real IDs are 101-105 and stay
fixed across saves. The wallet still holds five selections. Save/load can use
the existing validated format because it stores IDs, not financial terms.
The new default save should be separate from the old sample wallet.

Six planned purchase examples

1. Eligible supermarket $100: Savor $3 and Blue Cash Everyday $3, if its
   grocery allowance is unused. Those two tie.
2. Dining $50: Savor and Freedom Unlimited each $1.50. Both win.
3. Eligible U.S. gas $60: Blue Cash Everyday $1.80 with unused gas allowance.
4. Drugstore $40: Freedom Unlimited $1.20.
5. Other purchase $80: Active Cash $1.60.
6. Hotel $200 through Capital One Travel: Quicksilver and Savor each $10.
Also check the same hotel through Chase Travel, the cap-crossing $0.50,
an exhausted cap, unknown cap use, date boundaries, and invalid input.

Research conclusion

The earning rules and conditions needed for this Gate 4 scope are supported
by the issuer sources above. Some full pricing disclosures remain unavailable
and are named as unconfirmed rather than guessed. This research was finished
before adding the Gate 4 code. It does not certify every benefit or later
account change, and software tests cannot prove a bank's future terms.
